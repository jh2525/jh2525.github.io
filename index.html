I am an Integrated M.S./Ph.D. student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Korea University, advised by Prof. Byung-Jun Lee. My research centers on reinforcement learning — including offline RL, diffusion-based planning, and multi-agent RL — as well as preference optimization and safety for large generative models.
